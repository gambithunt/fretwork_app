# Fretwork privacy policy

_Last updated: 2026-10-02_

Fretwork is made by Delon Misic. Questions: **support@fretwork.org**.

## The short version

Fretwork does not collect your data. The microphone audio is analysed on your
device and is never recorded, stored, or sent anywhere. There is no account, no
advertising, and no tracking.

## Microphone

Fretwork asks for microphone access so it can hear your guitar. Audio is
processed in memory, on your device, to work out the note you played. It is
discarded immediately after analysis. No audio is saved and no audio is
transmitted.

You can deny or revoke microphone access at any time in **Settings → Privacy &
Security → Microphone**. Fretwork will tell you detection is unavailable and
offer a link back to Settings; everything else in the app still works.

## What Fretwork stores on your device

Fretwork saves your own preferences — sensitivity, tuning, board orientation,
which lesson you last opened, and similar settings — in the app's local
storage on your device. Those preferences stay on your device. They are not
sent to us and are not backed up to any Fretwork server. Deleting the app
deletes them.

## What Fretwork sends off your device

Nothing. The iOS app has no analytics, no crash-reporting SDK, no advertising
SDK, and no server to send to.

The Mac build downloaded directly from fretwork.org has an optional anonymous
usage pulse that is **off by default** and only sends the app version and an
approximate country when you explicitly turn it on. It never includes audio,
notes, practice history, device information, or any identifier that follows
you. The App Store (iOS) app does not include this at all.

## Purchases

The optional one-time unlock of the learning modules is handled entirely by
Apple through the App Store. We never see your name, payment card, or billing
address. Apple's handling of that purchase is covered by
[Apple's privacy policy](https://www.apple.com/legal/privacy/). You can restore
a previous purchase from the paywall or from Settings.

## Children

Fretwork is suitable for all ages and collects no personal information from
anyone, including children.

## Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be published at this address with
