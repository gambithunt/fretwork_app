# Fretwork support

Need help, found a bug, or have a suggestion? Email
**support@fretwork.org** and include your device model and iOS version.

## Getting started

1. Open Fretwork. It starts on the **Listen** screen.
2. Tap **Start** and allow microphone access when iOS asks.
3. Hold your phone near the guitar and play a single note. The note, its octave
   and its fretboard position appear. Strum a chord and it is named too.

For reliable detection, play one note at a time at first, keep the phone within
a metre or so, and avoid a very noisy room. An acoustic guitar works
unamplified; an electric guitar works best unplugged and close to the phone.

## Free and paid

**Free:** Listen (live note and chord detection) and the **Notes on the
fretboard** lesson.

**One-time purchase:** a single non-consumable unlocks the other nine lessons —
Intervals, Octaves, Triads, Chords, Pentatonic, Scales, Harmonizing, Note
association, and Circle of fifths. There is no subscription and nothing
renews. Purchases are handled by Apple.

## Common questions

**The app can't hear anything.**
Check **Settings → Privacy & Security → Microphone** and make sure Fretwork is
allowed. Then reopen the app and tap Start again.

**I bought the modules on another device and they're locked.**
Tap **Restore Purchases** on the paywall or in **Settings**. Make sure you are
signed in with the same Apple ID you bought with.

**I want a refund.**
Apple handles all App Store payments and refunds. Request one at
[reportaproblem.apple.com](https://reportaproblem.apple.com).

**Does Fretwork record me?**
No. Audio is analysed on your device and discarded. See the
[privacy policy](https://fretwork.org/privacy).

**The screen turns off while I'm practising.**
Turn on **Keep screen on while listening** in Settings.
